Data Deletion

Last updated: July 2026

Delete From Your Dashboard

Sign in at carpedmstrategies.co.uk, open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, all connected social-account access tokens, and your personal data within 30 days.

Disconnect a Single Platform

To remove only one connected platform (for example Facebook or Instagram) without closing your account, open Settings, then Connected Accounts, and select Disconnect next to that platform. The access token for that platform is deleted immediately.

Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account, email info@carpedmstrategies.co.uk from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will verify your identity and complete deletion within 30 days, then confirm by email.

Revoke Access From the Platform

You can also remove our access directly: in Facebook or Instagram, go to Settings, then Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), and remove Carpe DM; in LinkedIn, go to Settings, then Data Privacy, then Permitted Services. Revoking there stops all future access. Email us if you also want existing stored data erased.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile and login credentials
  • All stored access tokens for connected social accounts
  • Content, captions and scheduling data held on your behalf

We may retain limited records where required for legal, tax or accounting obligations; these are deleted when no longer required.
