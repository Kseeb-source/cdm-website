Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is the content actually good, or obvious AI filler?

It is genuinely good. We research your real business and write proper, structured articles with a clear answer up front, the questions your customers actually ask, internal links to your own pages, and a real featured image. Before you pay a penny, we generate two sample articles for your business so you can judge the quality yourself.

Do I have to do anything each week?

No. That is the whole point. You set it up once and articles appear on your site on schedule. If you would rather review each one before it goes live, that is a simple toggle. Otherwise it runs entirely on its own.

Will Google penalise AI-assisted content?

No, and this is a common myth. Google rewards helpful, expert content whoever or whatever drafts it. Our articles are built to that standard, fact-checked, and written to answer real search questions, which is exactly what Google and AI assistants look for.

What do I need for it to work?

A website you can publish to, most commonly WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix or Ghost. You connect it once during signup. If you are not sure what you have, start the free trial and we will check for you.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes. Autopilot works for any English-language business anywhere, UK, US and worldwide. You are billed in your local currency and any tax is handled automatically at checkout.

What about the social channels?

The blog engine is live today. Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X and Google Business Profile publishing are rolling out and switch on as add-ons you connect in one click, from £39 a channel per month. You never re-onboard, they simply appear as options in your dashboard.

Am I locked in?

Never. No contract, cancel in one click, and everything we publish stays on your site and belongs to you. The first month is free, so there is no risk in trying it.
