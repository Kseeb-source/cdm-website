Common questions

Everything you need
to know before we start.

We do not lock anyone into long contracts or surprise invoices. The answers below are the same ones we give on strategy calls.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most standard websites are live within 48 hours of sign-off. More complex sites with custom features, integrations or multiple page types typically take 5 to 7 days. You get a clear timeline in writing before we start — no drift.

Do I need to sign a long contract?

No. All services are month-to-month. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. No lock-ins, no cancellation fees, no penalties. You own your domain, your data and your integrations from day one.

Can you really get my business shown in AI search?

Yes. As buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers for recommendations, we make sure your business is one of the names that comes back.

It is the same authority work that wins Google rankings, your site, content and entity signals, pointed at the new search surface so the engines recognise and cite you.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most clients see measurable ranking improvements within 60 to 90 days. Significant traffic growth typically comes at the 3 to 6 month mark as content compounds and authority builds. Anyone promising overnight rankings is either paying for ads or not telling the truth.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We can add SEO, content and AI-visibility work to most existing sites. If your current site is holding you back technically, slow, not mobile-friendly, poor architecture, we will tell you honestly and give you the options. No pressure to rebuild if it is not needed.

Do you work with businesses outside London?

Yes — we work remotely with clients across the United Kingdom. Communication runs on email, WhatsApp and video call. Location does not matter. The stack is the same whether you're in London, Manchester or Aberdeen.

Can you get my business found in AI search like ChatGPT?

Yes. We get your business named and cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers. That means making sure the AI crawlers can read your site, publishing clear answer-shaped content with the right schema, building a consistent business identity, and earning mentions on the sources these models trust. See our full guide on how to get found in AI search.

What is AI search visibility (GEO)?

AI search visibility, also called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) or AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), is the practice of getting your business named inside AI-generated answers rather than just ranking in a list of links. It is the AI-era version of SEO, and it is where a growing share of buyers now start.

Do you do AI search visibility as well as SEO and Google Ads?

Yes, and they work best together. Most AI search still pulls from Google, so strong SEO feeds your AI visibility, while paid ads win the buyers who are ready now. We build all three as one connected system rather than separate line items.
