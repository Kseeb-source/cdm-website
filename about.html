About the company

Built to make ambitious
businesses impossible
to miss.

Carpe DM Strategies Ltd is a London-based growth marketing agency. We help ambitious businesses get found and chosen, across Google, paid and the new AI search engines, and we run the exact same playbook on our own companies.

Our story

Built from experience,
not just theory.

Carpe DM Strategies started because we watched too many good businesses stay invisible online. Not because they were not good enough, but because nobody was doing the work to get them found by the people, and now the AI engines, that decide who gets the call.

So we built the playbook ourselves and proved it on our own sister company Damp & Mould Solutions UK, a specialist property-care business operating across London. We took it from invisible to found, from the first Google click to the booked job.

Now we run that same playbook for other ambitious businesses: a fast, high-converting website, SEO and content that rank, Google Ads that pay back, and the new edge of getting named by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers.

One team, one playbook, one point of accountability. Built in London, for ambitious businesses across the UK.

Platform purpose

What this platform does.

The Carpe DM Strategies platform at www.carpedmstrategies.co.uk is an owned-and-operated business website. Here is exactly what it is used for, and how it fits together with the services we deliver.

01 — Public site

Market our own services

This website describes the services we offer, showcases the systems we build, and explains how we work. Visitors can read about our approach, browse FAQs, and get in touch.

02 — Lead capture

Receive and qualify enquiries

Prospective clients submit enquiries through the contact form. Each enquiry is routed to our team, where we qualify it, prepare a proposal and book a strategy call.

03 — Advertising

Google Ads & Google Ads API

We run Google Ads campaigns to reach UK businesses that may benefit from our services. We use the Google Ads API only to manage, report on and optimise our own advertising accounts and the advertising accounts of our direct clients — never resold, never exposed to third parties.
