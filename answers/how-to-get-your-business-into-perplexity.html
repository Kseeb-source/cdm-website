How do I get my business to show up in Perplexity?

AI assistants now hand out shortlists, and Perplexity is one of the most used. If it names a competitor when someone asks about your field, you are losing introductions before a human ever sees your website.

The short answer

Perplexity runs a live web search, then quotes the pages it trusts. To appear, publish clear pages that answer the exact questions buyers ask, keep your facts identical across every profile and listing, and earn mentions from sources Perplexity already reads. Then check your own questions monthly and fix whatever gets you skipped.

How Perplexity decides who to name

Perplexity answers by running a live web search, reading the strongest results, then quoting the pages it trusts. It does not keep a secret list of favoured brands. It reads the open web each time someone asks. To show up, become the clearest, best sourced page for the exact question a buyer types. If your site is thin, vague or slow, a competitor gets named in your place.

This matters because Perplexity users are often close to a decision. They ask for a shortlist, a comparison or a recommendation, then act on the names they see. Being absent is not neutral. It hands the introduction to whoever answered the question well. The good news is that the work rewards you across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Copilot too, because they all read similar signals.

  • Live retrieval: Perplexity fetches current pages, so recent, accurate content wins.
  • Citations: it shows its sources, so it favours pages it can quote cleanly.
  • Clarity: plain answers near the top of a page are easier to lift.

Make your facts identical everywhere

Fix your core facts first, because Perplexity cross checks what it reads. Your business name, what you do, the places you serve, your contact details and your main services should match on your own site, your Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, industry directories and any listing that mentions you. When these disagree, the model lowers its confidence and reaches for a source it trusts more. Consistency is boring, and it is the single cheapest way to raise your odds.

Write these facts in plain sentences a machine can copy. Instead of a slogan, state what you sell, who you help and where. "We are a payroll firm serving small businesses across the US and Canada" is quotable. "We reimagine payroll" is not. Put this near the top of your homepage and your about page. Repeat the same phrasing on your profiles so the story lines up wherever the crawler lands.

Keep a short reference document listing your approved facts, and update every profile when something changes. A moved office, a new service or a rebrand should be pushed everywhere within days, not months. Stale listings are a common reason a business gets described wrongly or skipped. Treat your online facts like stock records. If they are out of date, the answers built on them will be wrong too.

Publish pages that answer real questions

Build pages around the actual questions your customers ask, one clear question per page. Use the question as the heading, then answer it in the first two lines before you add context. This is the format Perplexity finds easiest to quote. Cover the practical things people search: how your service works, what it suits, how to choose between options and what to expect. Specific, useful pages get cited. Brochure pages that only praise the company do not.

Write down what prospects ask on calls and in emails. Those exact phrasings are what people type into Perplexity.

Open each page with a direct two line answer, then explain the detail underneath for readers who want it.

Name tools, timeframes and steps. Concrete detail is what a model can quote with confidence.

Refresh these pages on a schedule, because Perplexity favours current information. Review your main answers every few months, update anything that changed and add new questions as they come up. Link related pages together so a reader, and a crawler, can move from one answer to the next. Over time this builds a small library of quotable material that covers your topic more fully than a single sales page ever could.

Let the crawler read your site

Check that Perplexity can actually reach your pages, because none of the writing matters if the crawler is blocked. Perplexity uses a bot called PerplexityBot, and it reads your robots.txt file before fetching anything. Make sure that file does not disallow it, and that important pages are not hidden behind logins or heavy scripts. Many sites accidentally block useful crawlers while trying to stop scrapers. A five minute check can undo months of lost visibility.

Favour content that loads as plain text in the page source. If your key answers only appear after JavaScript runs, some crawlers miss them. Server rendered or static pages are safer. Keep page titles descriptive, use real headings rather than styled images, and make sure each page has a clear, unique purpose. Fast pages also help, because a crawler that times out simply moves on to a competitor who loaded quickly.

  • robots.txt: confirm PerplexityBot and common AI crawlers are allowed.
  • Rendering: put core answers in the HTML, not only in scripts.
  • Structure: one clear topic and one real heading per page.

Earn mentions from sources it trusts

Get named on pages that Perplexity already reads, because outside sources carry more weight than your own claims. Aim for genuine coverage: industry publications, respected directories, podcast notes, expert round ups and honest reviews on third party sites. When several credible pages describe you the same way, the model treats that description as reliable and repeats it. This is slower than editing your homepage, and it is what separates a cited business from an ignored one.

Pursue mentions you can actually earn. Offer useful commentary to journalists, contribute a clear guest article, ask satisfied clients to leave reviews on the platforms they already use, and make sure any partner or supplier who lists members includes you correctly. Quality beats volume. A handful of trusted, accurate pages does more than dozens of thin listings, and it lowers the risk of a wrong fact spreading across the web.

Watch how these sources describe you, not just whether they link. Perplexity often quotes the sentence a source uses, so an inaccurate line can travel far. When you spot an error, ask for a correction. When a source describes you well, point your own pages at it and reinforce the same wording. Over months this alignment makes it easy for any assistant to summarise your business in a way you would recognise.

Check whether it is working

Test your own questions in Perplexity regularly, because that is the only honest measure. Ask the things a customer would ask, in a few different ways, and note when you appear, which pages get cited and how you are described. Do this at least once a month, and keep a simple log. Answers shift as the web changes, so a single check tells you little. A trend over time tells you plenty.

Pay attention to the gaps. If a competitor is named and you are not, read the page Perplexity cited and ask what it answers that yours does not. Often the winning page is simply clearer or more specific. If your facts are quoted wrongly, trace the source and fix it. Treat every answer as feedback that points to the next page to write or the next listing to correct.

Expect this to take time, not days. New and updated pages need to be crawled, and trust builds as more sources agree. Keep publishing clear answers, keep your facts aligned and keep earning honest mentions. The businesses that show up in Perplexity are rarely the loudest. They are the ones that made themselves the easiest to quote accurately, and then kept that true as their market changed.

Frequently asked
How long does it take to show up in Perplexity?
Expect weeks to a few months, not days. Perplexity has to crawl your updated pages, and trust grows as more sources describe you the same way. Clear facts and quotable answers speed this up, but there is no switch that makes you appear instantly.
Can I pay Perplexity to feature my business?
No, you cannot buy your way into Perplexity's cited answers. It selects sources from live search based on relevance and trust. The reliable path is clear, accurate pages, consistent facts across the web and genuine mentions from sources it already reads.
Does Perplexity use the same sources as ChatGPT?
Not identical, but they overlap heavily. Both read the live web, favour clear pages and value trusted third party mentions. Work done to appear in Perplexity usually helps in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Copilot too, which is why the effort pays off broadly.
Why does Perplexity recommend my competitor instead of me?
Usually because their page answers the question more clearly, or more sources describe them consistently. Read the page Perplexity cited and compare. Often the fix is a plainer answer, more specific detail and correcting listings where your facts appear wrong or missing.
What is PerplexityBot and should I allow it?
PerplexityBot is the crawler Perplexity uses to read web pages before it can cite them. Yes, allow it in your robots.txt file if you want to appear. Blocking it, often by accident while stopping scrapers, is a common reason a business never shows up.
